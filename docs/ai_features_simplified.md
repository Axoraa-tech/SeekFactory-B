# 🚀 SeekFactory AI Feature Capabilities — Problem, Solution & Examples

> **Clean Executive PDF Document:** [SeekFactory_AI_Features_Simplified.pdf](./SeekFactory_AI_Features_Simplified.pdf)

---

### 1. AI Sourcing Concierge ("Ask SeekFactory") [Level 1: Core]
* **Problem:** Standard search boxes fail because industrial requirements have strict engineering parameters (tolerance, machine type, materials) that keywords cannot capture.
* **Solution:** A conversational assistant that understands technical parameters and instantly matches verified factories from the database.
* **Example:** A buyer types: *"Need 5-axis CNC machining for aerospace aluminum 7075 with ±0.005mm tolerance for 500 pcs."* → AI immediately shows 3 verified factories with DMG MORI 5-axis machines ready to quote.

---

### 2. Real-time Technical B2B Chat Translation (EN/HI ⇄ ZH) [Level 1: Core]
* **Problem:** Indian buyers and Chinese plant engineers face language barriers. Generic tools like Google Translate mistranslate specialized manufacturing jargon, causing defective production runs.
* **Solution:** In-chat translation that accurately translates specialized manufacturing terminology in both directions in real time.
* **Example:** Buyer types: *"Ensure wire EDM surface roughness is Ra 0.8 and confirm anodizing thickness in microns."* → Factory engineer instantly reads accurate technical Mandarin (线切割 Ra 0.8 & 阳极氧化膜厚度).

---

### 3. Smart RFQ Builder (Spec & Drawing Extractor) [Level 2: Advanced]
* **Problem:** Writing RFQs is tedious and complex; over 60% of buyers abandon the form or submit incomplete details (missing Incoterms, MOQ, tolerances).
* **Solution:** Buyers upload an engineering drawing (PDF/image) or rough notes; AI extracts technical specs and pre-fills the entire RFQ form automatically.
* **Example:** Buyer uploads a 2D PDF drawing of a valve block → AI automatically fills: Product Name: *"Hydraulic Valve Block"*, Material: *Ductile Iron GGG40*, Tolerance: *H7*, and suggested Incoterm: *FOB Ningbo*.

---

### 4. Reels Video Auto-Transcription & Bilingual Subtitles [Level 2: Advanced]
* **Problem:** Chinese factory managers narrate machine demonstrations in Mandarin, making video reels uninformative to Indian and international buyers.
* **Solution:** AI transcribes Mandarin audio, generates synchronized English subtitles directly on the video, and creates English titles and category tags.
* **Example:** A manager narrates a 600-ton die-casting test in Mandarin → Video displays English subtitles: *"Our 600-ton die casting unit produces aluminum transmission cases at 45s cycle times."* with *#DieCasting* tags.

---

### 5. Supplier Quotation Comparator & Recommendation [Level 2: Advanced]
* **Problem:** Buyers get overwhelmed comparing 3–5 quotes with different shipping terms (.20 FOB Ningbo vs .60 CIF Mumbai) and different delivery times.
* **Solution:** An automated decision assistant that standardizes quotes side-by-side and recommends the best supplier based on price, speed, and reliability.
* **Example:** Buyer receives 4 quotes for 2,000 shafts → AI highlights: *"Supplier B offers the lowest price (.80/unit), but Supplier A delivers 14 days faster with verified ISO 9001 rating. Recommendation: Choose A for urgent delivery, B for cost savings."*

---

### 6. Visual "Snap to Source" (Photo Part Matcher) [Level 3: Enterprise]
* **Problem:** Plant engineers frequently hold a broken machine part in hand, but have no part number, drawing, or technical term to search for it online.
* **Solution:** The user takes a photo of the physical component with the mobile app; AI identifies the part type, manufacturing method, and metal alloy, finding matching suppliers immediately.
* **Example:** Engineer snaps a photo of a broken loom gear → AI identifies: *"Spiral Bevel Gear, Module 3.5, Alloy Steel"* and instantly displays 4 verified gear-manufacturing plants capable of producing replacements.

---

### 7. India–China Landed Cost & Customs Duty Calculator (HS Code AI) [Level 3: Enterprise]
* **Problem:** Indian buyers hesitate to purchase from China due to fear of unexpected port customs duties, freight surcharges, and IGST taxes.
* **Solution:** AI assigns the correct 8-digit Indian Customs HS Code and calculates the total landed cost in Indian Rupees (INR) delivered to the buyer's factory gate.
* **Example:** A fiber laser machine is listed at **,000 FOB Shanghai** → AI itemizes freight, Basic Customs Duty (7.5%), SWS, and IGST to calculate: *"Total Landed Cost: ₹15,67,307 INR (₹2,39,080 claimable as GST credit)."*

---

### 8. Video Reels Personalization Engine [Level 3: Enterprise]
* **Problem:** B2B buyers have narrow industrial interests. A textile plant buyer will leave the platform if their video feed is flooded with plastic molding or mining equipment.
* **Solution:** An algorithm that observes buyer watch time, pauses on machine specs, and past RFQs to personalize their "For You" video feed with relevant industrial equipment.
* **Example:** A buyer pauses on CNC Swiss lathe reels and recently requested quotes for precision pins → Feed adapts within 24 hours to feature micro-machining and precision turning demonstrations.
